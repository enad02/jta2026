Need Help?

Do not struggle in silence. For normal teaching, stage, or mentoring questions, start with your usual mentor or academy route.

If the issue involves fairness, safeguarding, welfare, serious professional concern, or something that should not wait, raise it through the approved Jothi escalation route immediately.
